Sentiment Analysis of TikTok Reviews Using Lexicon-Based and Machine Learning(April 2025)

Deep Learning Project — Coding Camp powered by DBS Foundation 2025

This project analyzes 10,000 TikTok app reviews from the Google Play Store to identify user sentiment. The process includes text preprocessing, lexicon-based sentiment scoring, word visualization, and exploration of positive–negative opinions using WordCloud and Treemap.

📊 Datasets

  • Scrapping Google Play Store Review Dataset (10,000 records)

🛠️ Tools & Libraries

🧩 Project Stages

  • Data Loading & Cleaning :
      • ➡️ Removed missing values.
      ➡️ Standardized columns such as reviewCreatedVersion and appVersion.
      ➡️ Handled the limited replyContent values.
  • Text Preprocessing :
      • ➡️ Cleaning (remove numbers, mentions, RT, URL, punctuation)
      ➡️ Case folding
      ➡️ Slang normalization (e.g., ga → tidak, bgt → banget)
      ➡️ Tokenizing
      ➡️ Stopword removal (Indonesian + English)
      ➡️ Stemming (Sastrawi)
      ➡️ Rebuild the cleaned sentence
  • Sentiment Labeling (Lexicon-Based):
      • ➡️ Used positive & negative word dictionaries (CSV from GitHub)
      ➡️ Scoring based on word occurrence weights.
      ➡️ Ensuring all numerical fields are valid.
      ➡️ Three sentiment labels : Positive, Negative, Neutraal
      ➡️ Result Distribution : Positive: 7,211 Negative: 2,340 Neutral: 449
  • Visualization :
      • ➡️ Sentiment Distribution Pie Chart : Shows the dominance of positive sentiment in TikTok reviews.
      ➡️ WordClouds : All reviews, Positive-only, Negative-only, and Neutral-only.
      ➡️ Treemap of Top Words : Highlights the most frequent words in each sentiment category.
      ➡️ Bar Charts Top 15 most common words from : All comments, Positive comments, Negative comments, Neutral comments.
  • Business Insights :
      • ➡️ Peak usage occurs during commuting hours (morning & early evening).
      ➡️ Summer & fall show the highest demand.
      ➡️ Bad weather significantly reduces usage.

🎯 Results

  • The majority of reviews are positive : Most users praise TikTok’s features, experience, and inspirational content.
  • Negative comments are mainly about : Bugs, App lag, Login issues, Failed updates.
  • Most frequent words include : “tiktok”, “bagus”, “komentar”, “update”, “blokir”, “donlod”.
  • The complete visualizations and lexicon-based model successfully classify sentiments, providing valuable insights into TikTok user experience and common issues.
